Before you battle

How do I move and attack?

Move with WASD or the arrow keys and aim with the mouse. Left click fires your basic attack. Right click uses your second skill. Space dashes, Q uses your heavy attack, and E activates your ultimate. Switch with R or 1–3. Touch controls appear on phones.

Where is my progress saved?

Your teams, settings and challenge progress stay in this browser. Use Export save in Settings to make a backup or move your local progress to another device.

How does Online work here?

The Online screen opens poke2darena.com for multiplayer. Its server currently accepts the official site's domain. Your teams and saves on this community build are separate from your official game progress.

Who made this version?

This is an independently implemented community rebuild with original local battle mechanics and arena artwork. Poke2D Arena is developed by alex at poke2darena.com. Pokémon belongs to its respective rights holders.
