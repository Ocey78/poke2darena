Guides

Get started with this independently built local arena. Its battle mechanics and challenge stages are its own implementation.

Switch teammate
R or 1–3

On a phone, use the on-screen movement and attack controls. Choose from 1,025 species and keep three saved teams. Vs CPU offers three difficulties and six visual arenas. Story Mode contains sixteen local challenge stages.

Teams, settings, and challenge progress save in this browser. Use the save export in Settings to make a backup. Online opens the official game for live rooms, where your progress is separate.
